# Sound credits

Music and the remaining tones (heal, buff, badge, crit, fanfare, death, wagon, wind,
fire, thunder, whip, whistle, rattle, hiss, laugh, puff, dart, heartbeat) are generated
by `tools/gen_audio.py`. The recorded sound effects (`*_N.ogg`) are built by
`tools/import_sfx.py` from these free packs:
