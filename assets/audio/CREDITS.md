# Sound credits

Music and the weapon, impact and tone sounds (gunshot, rifle, shotgun, explosion, punch,
blunt, hit, slash, whoosh, clang, hammer, stomp, breaking, bell, howl, heal, buff, badge,
crit, fanfare, death, wagon, wind, fire, thunder, whip, whistle, rattle, hiss, laugh, puff,
dart, heartbeat, and the UI click) are generated by `tools/gen_audio.py`. The recorded sound effects
(`*_N.ogg`) are built by `tools/import_sfx.py` from these free packs:

| Sounds | Source | Author | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| glass, knock | [Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds) | Kenney | CC0 |
| cloth, coin, page, lantern, footsteps | [RPG Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio) | Kenney | CC0 |
| coin, card | [Casino Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio) | Kenney | CC0 |
| paper | [100 CC0 SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx) | rubberduck | CC0 |
| eat, roar, yip | [80 CC0 creature SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-creature-sfx) | rubberduck | CC0 |
| bite, eerie | [RPG Sound Pack](https://opengameart.org/content/rpg-sound-pack) | artisticdude | CC0 |
| caw | [Crow Caw](https://opengameart.org/content/crow-caw) | zeroisnotnull | CC0 |
| caw | [Crow](https://opengameart.org/content/crow) | fvcalderan | CC0 |
| growl | [Dog snarl, grunt, grumble](https://opengameart.org/content/dog-snarl-grunt-grumble) | qubodup | CC0 |
| growl | [Wolf monster sound](https://opengameart.org/content/wolf-monster-sound) | CaveboyTup | CC0 |
| roar | [Big Scary Troll Sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/big-scary-troll-sounds) | Darsycho | CC0 |
| dog | [Dog Sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/dog-sounds) | pauliuw | CC0 |
| screech | [Bat Screeches](https://opengameart.org/content/bat-screeches) | AntumDeluge | CC0 |

Loudness: `tools/level_audio.py` measures every clip and writes `levels.json`, the per-clip
gain the game applies at play time.
